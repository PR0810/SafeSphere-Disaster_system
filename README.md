# SafeSphere – Disaster Preparedness and Response Education System

SafeSphere is an educational App & Web platform designed to help students and educators learn disaster preparedness, safety, and emergency response through interactive and engaging learning.

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🎥 **Interactive Learning** – Videos, quizzes, simulations, and micro-modules for disaster training.
* 🧠 **Resilience & Wellbeing** – Activities and resources to help students cope with stress and build emotional resilience after disasters.
* 🤖 **AI Risk Alerts** – Location-based weather and disaster notifications.
* 🥽 **Immersive XR Drills** – AR/VR-based training for earthquake, fire, and flood scenarios.
* 📚 **Resource Hub** – Verified safety guidelines, emergency contacts, alerts, and SOS resources.
* 🎮 **Gamification & Certification** – Badges, leaderboards, and blockchain-based certificates.
* 🌐 **Inclusive Access** – Multilingual, offline, and cross-device support.
* 👩‍🏫 **Curriculum Integration** – Teacher dashboards and student performance tracking.
* 📱 **Offline Mode** – Access learning modules and important resources without internet.
* ☁️ **Future-Ready Technology** – Uses AI, IoT, cloud technologies, and gamification.

## 🎯 Objective

To make disaster preparedness education accessible, interactive, inclusive, and practical for students and educators while promoting safety, resilience, and wellbeing.

## 🛠️ Technologies

* AI / Machine Learning
* IoT
* AR/VR (XR)
* Cloud
* Blockchain
* Web & Mobile Technologies

## 🌟 Innovation

SafeSphere combines disaster education with AI-powered alerts, immersive drills, gamification, offline learning, and resilience-focused wellbeing support to create a future-ready disaster preparedness platform.
